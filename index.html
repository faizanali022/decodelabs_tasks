About Us

Family-Owned. Manufacturer-Direct. Built on Craftsmanship.

Lux EcoShades LLC. is a family-owned manufacturer and installer of high-end custom window shades and window solutions, proudly serving homeowners and businesses throughout the Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex.

Unlike many companies, we don't simply resell standard window coverings. We manufacture our own custom roller shades, built according to the exact dimensions, design preferences, and functional requirements of each project.
